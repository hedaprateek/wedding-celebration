# Details to make this wedding invitation yours

Known so far: **Prerna**, **1 and 2 December**, **Marwari wedding**. The groom's name can follow later. The year, schedule, venue and photographs currently shown are provisional.

Copy this list and fill in whatever you have. Unknown details can wait.

1. **Names and dates** — Bride and groom's full names and preferred spelling; wedding year; preferred wedding title or hashtag.
2. **Family and invitation** — Parents' and grandparents' names, family names, who is inviting, a personal welcome, blessings, or a quotation you want included.
3. **The actual programme** — Every function you want listed, date, start/end time, venue, baraat and phera timings/muhurat, family-specific Marwari customs, dress codes and any restricted invitations. Say which parts are still tentative.
4. **Venues** — Name, city, full address and Google Maps link for each venue; a venue photo; parking and accessibility information.
5. **Your photographs and videos** — Around 10–20 favourite couple/family photos, a preferred hero photo, short captions, and any short video you have permission to share. Tell me which photos are private.
6. **Your story and personalities** — How you met, a few milestones, favourite shared memories, funny little facts, what each person loves about the other, and favourite quotes. Just a few honest sentences are enough.
7. **RSVP arrangements** — Deadline, host contact name and phone/email/WhatsApp, who should receive responses, guest limits, event choices and dietary/accommodation questions. Confirm how you want responses delivered before the site goes live.
8. **Travel and hospitality** — Hotel/stay arrangements, check-in dates, station/airport guidance, pickup plans, local family contacts and any useful tips for out-of-town guests.
9. **Language and atmosphere** — English, Hindi, Marwari or a mix; favourite colours; invitation artwork/monogram; any music you own or have permission to use; rituals or personal touches you especially want celebrated.

Real stories and photographs will make the biggest difference to how personal the website feels. The current sample photos and suggested programme should be replaced or confirmed before sharing the live invitation.
