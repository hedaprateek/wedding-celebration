    A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

    A little
    before you go.

    Are the venue and timings final?

    The dates are 1 and 2 December. The year is currently set to 2026 for this preview; the family will confirm the final year, ceremony timings, venue and muhurat before invitations are shared.

    What should I wear?

    Bring your festive favourites! Each celebration has a suggested dress mood on its event card. Comfortable footwear and a warm layer for December evenings are always a lovely idea.

    Can I bring my family?

    Please follow the guests included in your invitation and enter your full party count in the RSVP. For parties larger than eight, contact the family once their contact details are available.

    Can I update my RSVP?

    Send a new response using the same email or mobile number. Your latest response will update the previous one, so the guest count stays accurate.

    What about food and dietary needs?

    Tell the family about dietary requirements or allergies in your RSVP note. Menu and catering details will be confirmed by the hosts directly.
